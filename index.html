Our Story

A Masjid
Born from Community

Martella Masjid is a small, resident-run mosque located within the residential building at 1915 Martin Grove Road in Etobicoke. Founded by the Muslim residents of the building, it is a space of intimacy, devotion, and brotherhood.

We offer all five daily prayers in congregation, and during the blessed month of Ramadan, we hold nightly Taraweeh prayers for our community.
